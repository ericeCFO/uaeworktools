Privacy first

Privacy policy

The calculator platform is designed to work without accounts and without storing salary, employer, identity, or contact details.

No sensitive storage

Calculator inputs should run in the browser where possible and should not be saved to a database.

Analytics

Future analytics should track product events without collecting names, employer names, Emirates ID, phone numbers, or exact personal identifiers.

Contact forms

If contact forms are added later, they should collect only what is necessary to respond.
